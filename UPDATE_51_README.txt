NOVA UPDATE 51 — EXPANDABLE ON-WALL WIRING VIEW

Changes:
- Wired devices have an expand button (⤢).
- Click to enlarge the integrated open-box wiring 2.45x in place, with the selected device staying anchored to the wall.
- Expanded view is integrated into the wall workspace, not a separate detached overlay.
- Close button restores normal wall device size.
- Expanded device is not draggable while open, preventing accidental repositioning.
- Existing saved wiring and right-hand wiring panel are unchanged.

Limitations:

- Wires and terminal positions remain schematic.
- Expanded boxes may cover nearby wall devices until collapsed.
- Runtime visual testing was not performed.
